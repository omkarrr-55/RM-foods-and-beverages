# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Users:** Everyday beverage consumers across India encountering RM Foods products in retail shops, transit hubs, railway stations, or scanning bottle label QR codes. Their job is finding hygienic, trustworthy drinking water and refreshing regional flavoured soft drinks (Mango, Jeera, Nimbu Pani).
- **Secondary Users:** Retailers, regional distributors, and institutional buyers (railway catering contractors, event organizers, wholesale clients) looking to verify manufacturer credentials, facility capacity, and initiate distribution or co-packing inquiries.

## Product Purpose

RM Foods & Beverages is a Pune-based beverage manufacturer producing packaged drinking water and flavoured soft drinks. The product website exists to build consumer trust, celebrate regional Indian flavours, provide clear compliance/origin information via on-bottle QR codes, and facilitate direct commercial inquiries via WhatsApp and direct calling.

## Positioning

A verified, automated regional beverage manufacturer offering certified purity and authentic Indian taste profiles, distinguished by formal approvals across multiple Indian Railway zones (Central, Eastern, Western, and Konkan Railways), BIS/ISO 22000:2018 certifications, and high-speed 240 BPM bottling discipline.

## Operating Context

- **Consumer & Mobile Context:** High percentage of visits on mobile smartphones, often over variable cellular connections (scanned directly from product bottle QR codes on the move or via social channels).
- **Commercial & Trade Context:** Direct quick-order inquiries through pre-formatted WhatsApp chat links and immediate telephone contact rather than complex shopping carts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pure static responsive frontend: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 with modern custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript without client-side framework overhead.
- Interactive multi-state manufacturing and partner network map powered by Leaflet.js.
- Dedicated standalone label QR compliance pages (`qr-compliance.html`, `qr-softdrink-juice.html`) for regulatory transparency.
- WhatsApp message builder for trade and customer inquiries.
- Verified production floor video integration showcasing automated bottling processes.

## Product Principles

1. **Hygiene & Verification First:** Prioritize clear evidence of safety, certifications (BIS, FSSAI, ISO), and production standards to establish immediate consumer trust.
2. **Vibrant Flavour Celebration:** Treat Double Dhamaal fruit and spice profiles with appetite-inducing clarity and distinct regional Indian identity.
3. **Frictionless Mobile-First Experience:** Ensure fast load times, accessible layouts, and clear information hierarchy for on-the-go QR scanners.
4. **Direct, Human Communication:** Keep partnership and customer connection paths instant, accessible, and direct via WhatsApp and phone.
