Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or contact RM Foods & Beverages through WhatsApp, phone, or email.

Effective: 18 June 2026

This website does not create user accounts and does not operate a customer database. The business enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message only when you choose to continue.

1. Information you provide

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and optional phone number.
  • Your enquiry type and the requirement or message you enter.
  • Information you later share through WhatsApp, phone, email, or other communication.

2. How the enquiry form works

The form runs in your browser. When you select "Continue on WhatsApp," the website opens WhatsApp with a prepared message addressed to RM Foods & Beverages. The website itself does not submit that form to an RM Foods & Beverages server or database. WhatsApp handles the communication under its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Technical and third-party data

Our future hosting provider may record standard server information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access time for security and operational purposes. The website also loads or links to third-party services, including:

  • WhatsApp for business enquiries.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles and Leaflet for the manufacturing network map.
  • Google Maps for location directions.
  • YouTube image servers and YouTube links for process videos.
  • Instagram links for brand social accounts.
  • Google Fonts for website typography.

These providers may receive technical information when their content or services are accessed. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

4. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to distribution, supply, institutional, and co-packing enquiries.
  • Prepare quotations, discuss requirements, and manage business communications.
  • Protect the website and business from misuse or security threats.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, professional advisers, or authorities where reasonably necessary for business operations or required by law. Communications are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, business relationship, record keeping, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

6. Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. A cookie-consent banner is therefore not currently displayed. Third-party services may use their own technologies when you access their content or follow their links. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this policy and the consent approach will be updated.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to use the enquiry form and contact us by phone or email instead. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information held in our direct business communications, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

8. Security and external links

We use reasonable measures to protect information in our control, but no electronic method is completely secure. External websites and services are outside our control, and visitors should review their policies before providing information.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website, our services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest published version.
